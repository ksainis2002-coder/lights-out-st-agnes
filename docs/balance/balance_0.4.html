Why no human-pace number for wing 3

Played the way wing 1 was measured (15 s of looking around after every step), the fixed route keeps meeting Sister M.: a bot can't watch and hear her the way a player does, so its count of catches (10 in its first 32 minutes in one run) says more about the bot than the wing.
