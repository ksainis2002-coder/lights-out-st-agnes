Wing 4 (the chapel and the basement): the whole wing by its bot routes

Wing 4's three bot routes played one after another on Normal: the chapel and the crypt (A), the basement with Dr. Venn and Sam (B), Nora and the way out (C). The walks between the chapel, the west wing and the basement stairs are skipped. The flashlight is on throughout, as a player keeps it (it goes off only in a locker and when he knocks it aside).

As fast as the bot goes, Dr. Venn live: the whole wing in 6.3 minutes, never heard or caught, home with sanity 97 and Nora behind you: Acceptance.

At a human pace

The same routes with 15 s of looking around after every step, as a player reads a room; with St. Agnes in the room it keeps its eyes on her. Dr. Venn still walks and hunts, but a bot can't dodge him the way a player does, so when he reaches it he is stunned for 20 s instead of a rewind (11 times; not counted as deaths). A blackout from the dark puts sanity back to 60 on the spot. Dead batteries are swapped and counted.

What it shows

  • The wing finishes in one go: at bot pace nobody is caught and the run ends in Acceptance.
  • The basement has no light that steadies you: its lamps are too dim to count (sanity comes back only in light of 0.8 or more, and no basement spot reaches it), so even with the flashlight on it falls about 5 a minute. At a human pace a player blacks out there once and comes to the theatre low (here 11).
  • Freeing Nora (+30, then the dark at half) brings that player home at 25, short of 65. Acceptance needs about 50 as you free her, which nothing in the basement lets a player get back to without pills.
  • The flashlight burns 4 batteries over the wing; wing 4 has none to find and the whole game has 6, all in wings 1 and 2.
  • In the chapel, keeping St. Agnes in view costs sanity (99 over part A here, as the bot never looks away from her); a player who glances away loses less but lets her move.
